mod-dpssim healing report — generated by run-sim.sh, values baked in at generation time.
How to read this. A pin scenario (targets held at a fixed health by the sim) is the pure-HPS measurement: nobody fills up, overheal stays near zero and nobody dies. A drain scenario caps healing at the incoming damage, so read deaths, overheal and mana (when the healer ran out) instead of HPS. A mixed scenario shows triage.

Summary

Healing by spell

Healing counts effective heals plus the damage absorbed by the healer's shields; overheal is the part of a heal that landed on a full target. HPM is healing per mana of the spell id that cost the mana: a heal that lands under a different id than the cast (a proc, a bouncing heal) shows its mana on the cast row and its healing on the heal row.
